# Concept prompts

Tool: built-in image generation (not CLI).

## Initial generation

Create a polished original 3D game character art-direction board for TenAce, a create-your-own tennis player story RPG. This is concept art for a future modular 3D asset, not a screenshot of a working game. Landscape presentation on a warm pale gray studio background, restrained clean typography. Title exactly 'TENACE / YOUR PLAYER. YOUR STORY.' High-end playful mobile-game sculpt rendering with the cohesive exaggerated forms, confident silhouettes, appealing sculpted faces, rich material separation and soft global illumination associated with Clash Royale, but wholly original modern tennis athletes: no copied characters, no armor, no fantasy props. Slight anime influence, young ADULT athletes, roughly five-heads-tall proportions, athletic legs, expressive natural-sized eyes, shaped five-finger hands, beautifully fitted sportswear and chunky performance tennis shoes. Avoid creepy doll eyes, huge staring pupils, plastic mannequin faces, primitive capsule limbs or blocky low-poly shapes. Main left two-thirds: one customizable sample athlete with medium warm skin, short sculpted dark curls, relaxed confident closed-mouth smile, teal/navy tennis kit with tiny lime piping, neutral balanced standing pose, racket resting at side. Show SAME athlete at consistent scale in front, side and rear full-body views with identical garment seams and proportions. Small labels FRONT / SIDE / BACK. Right third: modular personalization panel headed 'MAKE IT YOURS' with four beautifully rendered bust examples using the same art direction but distinct face structure, skin tone and hair: dark skin with braided bun and feminine facial structure, fair freckled skin with swept auburn hair and masculine structure, East Asian facial features with black ponytail and feminine structure, medium brown skin with short textured hair and masculine structure. Include a tidy row of interchangeable headband, cap, tennis top and racket assets beneath those portraits, no body parts floating gruesomely. Across the bottom, a compact strip headed 'GROW INTO YOUR GAME': THREE small full-body renders of the SAME main athlete in progression order, labeled ROOKIE / CONTENDER / CHAMPION. Rookie wears a simple training kit and basic racket, contender a tailored club kit, champion refined premium performance kit and trophy at their feet. SAME recognizable face/body and age throughout, growing confidence and expressive posture, not muscles or body size as a level reward. Emphasize that the player creates their own identity, not selects a fixed named hero. Keep text sparse, readable and exactly as specified. Crisp professional character-development sheet, visually stunning sculpt quality, no phone frame, no skill trees, no UI buttons, no watermark.

## Final stylization edit

Edit this TenAce character concept board. Preserve its exact layout, text, front/side/back consistency, customizable diverse portrait identities, interchangeable tennis equipment, and rookie-to-champion progression of the same athlete. Change ONLY the art style and proportions across ALL characters: make them significantly more stylized, chunky, appealing premium 3D cartoon characters, with the strong readable silhouettes and sculpted volume of Clash Royale. Main adult athlete should be approximately FOUR AND A HALF heads tall, with a head 35 percent larger relative to the body, broader hands and shoes, compact athletic torso and shorter sturdy legs. Keep them visibly young adults, not babies. Faces must be expressive and appealing, strong graphic brows, simplified broad cheek planes, smaller simplified nose, natural relaxed eyes not oversized staring eyeballs. Hair is beautiful sculpted interlocking masses with a few purposeful grooves, NOT thousands of realistic strands. Clothing has a few large sculpted folds, no photographic fabric texture, no skin pores or body hair. Rackets and shoes use bold simplified rounded shapes. Beautiful smooth high-end 3D materials, soft bounce lighting, gentle ambient occlusion, rich teal navy and lime. Avoid realism, clay dolls, flat vectors, cheap primitive cylinder bodies, uncanny smiles, childish toddler proportions. This should unmistakably look like a premium animated mobile sports game with a unique original character lineup, NOT a realistic sports product photo. Preserve all readable headings and labels. All three turnaround poses must show the same new stylized athlete proportions.
