# TenAce: Road to #1 — Design Bible v0.2

## Product Pillar
A portrait-first tennis RPG where the player grows from unknown local competitor to world #1.

## Core Loop
Story -> Train -> Match -> Earn XP/Gear -> Upgrade -> Unlock Rival/Location -> Repeat

## Match Philosophy
- One-handed portrait play
- Semi-automatic movement
- Player agency is shot choice, timing, aim, risk, and tactics
- Stats should make execution easier, not automatically decide outcomes

## Prototype Controls
- Swipe toward target to hit
- Choose Topspin / Flat / Slice / Lob
- Serve uses timing meter
- Character auto-positions based on Speed + Tennis IQ

## Initial Stats
- Power: pace and depth
- Control: timing forgiveness and precision
- Speed: movement / recovery
- Tennis IQ: anticipation and tactical assists

## Chapter 1
Location: Riverdale Public Courts

Characters:
- Player: "The Unknown"
- Jax Mercer: Local Court King / first rival
- Coach: TBD, observes the Jax match and introduces progression

Story Beats:
1. Arrive at public courts
2. Learn basic hitting
3. Jax challenges the player
4. First-to-7 match
5. Earn XP and first upgrade
6. Coach approaches player
7. Unlock training court
8. Unlock first local tournament
