# Tennis character replacement — 2026-09-12

Superseded by the user’s decision to use original stylized characters in 0.18. No paid character or animation purchase is needed for this direction. The candidates below are historical research only.

The generic Quaternius character remains a prototype. Release 0.17 improves presentation but does not replace the character or deliver a new tennis animation set. No paid asset has been purchased.

## Candidate character

[Tennis Player with Racket and Ball — Rigged, by Hisenberg](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models/character/man/tennis-player-with-racket-and-ball-rigged)

The listing shows $50 before any applicable checkout charges, FBX/glTF/Blender formats, 11,082 polygons and 10,806 vertices. Its description lists tennis clothing, shoes, racket and ball and specifies a T-pose rig. These are publisher claims, not checks of purchased files. A finished tennis animation set is not established by “animation ready.” The glTF download is listed as 33.5 MB, so texture and runtime size need inspection before mobile integration. The preview image could not be retrieved through the research tool; visual approval remains outstanding. License is labeled “Royalty Free License (no AI)”; the applicable terms must be checked for the intended commercial game workflow before purchase. Do not assume that buying this model also solves animation.

## Candidate motion pack

[Tennis Animation Pack, by Jane Gintsar](https://www.fab.com/listings/972d6704-d991-4994-8c61-53d9f4174c97)

The publisher describes 14 clips covering serves, forehand/backhand, movement and ready stance. The listed format is Unreal Engine, so this is not yet a verified drop-in source for this Three.js game. Exportable FBX files, license, actual clip quality, skeleton compatibility and price remain unverified. No recommendation to purchase until those are resolved.

## Acceptance before replacing the default player

- Correct racket grip and contact at normal forehand/backhand/serve heights; no visible clothing intersections in those poses.
- Athletic proportions and actual tennis garments with distinct player/rival appearances.
- Prepared swing, weight transfer and recovery, including believable lateral foot placement.
- A phone rally with the correct shot trajectory, reliable touch input and no visual fallback errors.
- Measure frame rate and thermal behavior on a physical target phone. A desktop browser set to phone dimensions cannot establish this.

The user's asset budget is pending. Existing free assets and the playable preview remain available while the replacement decision is made.
