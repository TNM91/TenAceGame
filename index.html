Practice & swing guide
Original characters · Studio & play
Choose your serve placement, then tap in the green. Hold anywhere on court to load power, then swipe as the bounced return reaches you. Swipe up for topspin, sideways for flat, down for slice, or long and slowly up for a lob. Angle left or right to aim. Release without a swipe to cancel. Arrow keys are a desktop fallback. Two serve faults lose the point.
